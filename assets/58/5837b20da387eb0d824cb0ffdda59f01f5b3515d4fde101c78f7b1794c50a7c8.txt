OpenCV Vision and Control Parameters
Source: archived s2_preview_core_local.py and driving runner used in the verified trials.

Image acquisition and sampling
- Image resolution: 160 × 120 pixels
- Configured camera frame rate: 20 Hz
- Scan-row range: 55%–90% of image height
- Scan-row spacing: 2.5% of image height (approximately 3 pixels)
- Processing crop start: max(0, int(height × 0.55) − 6), or row 60 for a 120-pixel image

HSV colour thresholds
- White lower bound: [0, 0, 145]
- White upper bound: [179, 80, 255]
- Yellow lower bound: [15, 60, 80]
- Yellow upper bound: [40, 255, 255]

Morphological filtering
- White mask: opening with a 1-row × 3-column kernel; no closing
- Yellow mask: opening and closing with a 3 × 3 kernel

Boundary and lane checks
- Candidate segment width: 2 pixels to 18% of image width
- Minimum white-path support: 9 sampled points
- Dark-pixel threshold: V < 145
- Minimum dark-pixel proportion beside the white boundary: 80%
- Allowed yellow-to-white boundary spacing: 15%–85% of image width (24–136 pixels)
- Minimum checked lane-interior strip length: 8 pixels
- Minimum dark-pixel proportion within that strip: 90%
- Maximum number of candidate white paths considered: 64

Independent yellow-boundary fit (fallback)
- Polynomial degree: 2
- Minimum number of yellow anchors: 8
- Minimum vertical anchor span: 20% of image height
- Inlier residual threshold: 2.5% of image width (4 pixels)
- Minimum inlier proportion: 90%

Local lane-centre fit (fallback)
- Polynomial degree: 1
- Minimum number of centre points: 3
- Minimum vertical support span: 5% of image height
- Maximum extrapolation to the near target: 5% of image height
- Maximum absolute fit residual: 1.5 pixels
- Maximum estimated near-target uncertainty: 1.5 pixels
- Additional slope-uncertainty and candidate-agreement checks are applied

Target positions and steering
- Near-target row: 65% of image height (row 78)
- Far-target row: 55% preferred (row 66), otherwise 60% (row 72)
- Maximum interpolation span between supporting points: 15% of image height
- Image-centre x-coordinate used by the controller: 80 pixels
- Direction-term weight: 1.5
- Preview-error clipping range: −1 to +1
- Steering saturation scale: 0.5 in preview-error units
- Steering PWM centre: 365
- Steering PWM range: 290–440
- Fixed forward-throttle PWM request: 400
- Stopped-throttle PWM request: 370

Centre-offset calculation
- Lane centre: (white_x + yellow_x) / 2
- Normalized near offset: (near_x − 80) / 80
- Signed near offset in pixels: near_offset × 80
- Absolute near offset in pixels: abs(near_offset × 80)

Notes
- These are parameters of the archived tested implementation, not a live check of the Raspberry Pi.
- Image-space width limits are not physical lane-width measurements.
- The near-offset value is a controller estimate; formal evaluation and reference validation remain to be completed.
