Evidence-based conclusions

  • Completed-lap evidence: three separate OpenCV runs on 8 September finished a lap within the configured 16-second test window.
  • Descriptive comparison: all five listed runs per method on 9 September completed a lap. The operator recorded no line contacts for OpenCV and a mean of 1.8 contacts per CNN lap under those test conditions.
  • Sampled image error: OpenCV had lower RMSE values in the measurable samples, but the sample is small, missing frames are not random, and no physical lateral error was measured.
Interpretation

Sequential order, battery replacement and manual timing limit speed comparisons. Pixel marks were AI-assisted initially and visually reviewed by the operator; they are not independent blind ground truth. The demonstration videos are not precisely linked to every control log.

RESEARCH METHODS / FROM THE THESIS

How images become
steering decisions.

Selected methods and equations from Chapters 3–5 of the thesis draft. Each formula includes its variables, units and connection to the recorded experiments.

OPENCV / COLOUR AND GEOMETRY · §4.4

Extract the marked lane

The camera provides 160 × 120 images. HSV masks identify white and yellow markings. A 1 × 3 opening preserves thin white markings; the yellow branch uses 3 × 3 opening and closing. Boundary pairing checks width, continuity and dark road evidence before accepting a centre target.

xc(r) = [xy(r) + xw(r)] / 2

xy and xw are the horizontal yellow and white marking positions at relative image height r = y/H. Their midpoint xc is measured in pixels. Source: Eq. (4.1).

CNN / IMITATION LEARNING · §4.5

Predict steering from demonstrations

DonkeyCar KerasLinear learns from recorded camera images and driving commands. The preserved training record contains 39 epochs; the existing model is deployed in TensorFlow Lite format on the Raspberry Pi.

camera image → CNN → steering prediction

During comparison, local_angle mode uses learned steering with fixed throttle 0.375, equivalent to a request of 400 PWM counts. Validation loss measures fitting to recorded examples; it does not establish lap completion or lane clearance. Quantization and pruning are proposed extensions without measured improvements in this study.

IMAGE-SPACE OFFSETS · EQ. (4.2)

Measure near and far targets

en = [xc(rn) − W/2] / (W/2)
ef = [xc(rf) − W/2] / (W/2)

W = 160 pixels. The near row rn = 0.65 corresponds to y = 78. The far row rf = 0.55 corresponds to y = 66; rf = 0.60 is an alternative when the first location is unavailable.

Offsets are dimensionless. A positive value places the target to the right of the image centre.

PREVIEW CONTROL · EQS. (4.3)–(4.5)

Combine position and road direction

t = (ef − en) × 0.10 / (0.65 − rf)
ep = clip(en + 1.5t, −1, 1)
Ps = round[365 − 75 × clip(ep/0.5, −1, 1)]

The preview term t lets farther road direction influence steering. clip restricts a value to the stated interval. Ps is the requested steering PWM count, with centre 365 and limits 290–440.

These formulas apply when the required targets are available. The separately constrained local-fit fallback uses its fitted slope. PWM counts represent commands; wheel angle and physical speed were not measured.

SAMPLED PIXEL DEVIATION · EQ. (5.1)

Define a common evaluation reference

c = (xL + xR) / 2
e = c − 80

At row y = 78, xL and xR are reviewed marking-centre coordinates. c is their midpoint, and e is its signed distance from the common image-centre convention x = 80, in pixels.

Evaluation coordinates are reviewed separately from the controller’s target predictions. Negative e places the lane centre to the left. Marking gaps or ambiguous endpoints remain unmeasurable.

STATISTICAL MEASURES · EQS. (5.2)–(5.4)

Summarise measurable samples

MSE = (1/n) ∑i=1n ei2
RMSE = √MSE
MAE = (1/n) ∑i=1n |ei|

n counts measurable sampled images in a run. MSE has units px²; RMSE and MAE have units px. The chart above reports per-run RMSE for the six selected runs.

34 of 60 candidates are measurable. The 26 missing candidates are excluded without inserting zero errors. These statistics describe the measurable image subset and do not provide physical lateral distance or full-lap accuracy.

Control checks

Motion requires five consecutive valid, fresh frames. A target or freshness failure preserves a stop request. Image-age and control-update thresholds are 0.2 s and 0.25 s. The original OpenCV runner has a 16 s limit; the supplementary phone series uses a 30 s runner. These software requests depend on the actuator communication path.
