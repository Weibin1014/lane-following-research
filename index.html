Evidence-based conclusions

  • Completed-lap evidence: three separate OpenCV runs on 8 September finished a lap within the configured 16-second test window.
  • Descriptive comparison: all five listed runs per method on 9 September completed a lap. The operator recorded no line contacts for OpenCV and a mean of 1.8 contacts per CNN lap under those test conditions.
  • Sampled image error: OpenCV had lower RMSE values in the measurable samples, but the sample is small, missing frames are not random, and no physical lateral error was measured.

OPENCV / COLOUR AND GEOMETRY · §4.4

Extract the marked lane

The camera provides 160 × 120 images. HSV masks identify white and yellow markings. Boundary pairing checks width, continuity and dark road evidence before accepting a centre target.

LANE CENTRE
xc(r)=xy(r)+xw(r)2(4.1)

Definitionxy and xw are the horizontal yellow and white marking positions at relative image height r = y/H. Their midpoint is measured in pixels.

CNN / IMITATION LEARNING · §4.5

Predict steering from demonstrations

DonkeyCar KerasLinear learns from recorded camera images and driving commands. The preserved training record contains 39 epochs; the existing model is deployed in TensorFlow Lite format.

Camera image→CNN→Steering prediction

Evidence boundaryValidation loss measures agreement with recorded examples; it does not establish lap completion or lane clearance. The comparison used a fixed throttle request of 0.375.

IMAGE-SPACE OFFSETS · EQ. (4.2)

Measure near and far targets

NORMALIZED OFFSETS
en=xc(rn)−W2W2ef=xc(rf)−W2W2(4.2)

W = 160 px; rn = 0.65 corresponds to y = 78, while rf = 0.55 corresponds to y = 66.

InterpretationThe offsets are dimensionless. A positive value places the target to the right of the image centre.

PREVIEW CONTROL · EQS. (4.3)–(4.5)

Combine position and road direction

ROAD DIRECTION TERM
t=(ef−en)×0.100.65−rf(4.3)
PREVIEW ERROR
ep=clip(en+1.5t,−1,1)(4.4)
STEERING REQUEST
Ps=round[365−75×clip(ep0.5,−1,1)](4.5)

The preview term t lets farther road direction influence steering. Ps is the requested steering PWM count, with centre 365 and limits 290–440.

Evidence boundaryPWM counts are commands. Wheel angle and physical speed were not measured.

SAMPLED PIXEL DEVIATION · EQ. (5.1)

Define a common evaluation reference

COMMON REFERENCE
c=xL+xR2e=c−80(5.1)

At row y = 78, xL and xR are reviewed marking-centre coordinates. The signed error e is measured in pixels.

InterpretationNegative e places the lane centre to the left. Ambiguous samples remain unmeasurable.

n counts measurable sampled images. MSE has units px²; RMSE and MAE have units px.

Sample boundary34 of 60 candidates are measurable. Missing candidates are excluded without inserting zero errors.

Control checks

Motion requires five consecutive valid, fresh frames. A target or freshness failure preserves a stop request. Image-age and control-update thresholds are 0.2 s and 0.25 s. The original OpenCV runner has a 16 s limit; the supplementary phone series uses a 30 s runner. These software requests depend on the actuator communication path.
